How validation works

Chronological holdout: first 70% of observations train a single shrinkage calibration multiplier; final 30% test it out of sample. Reports MAE, RMSE, naive-baseline MAE and surge classification precision/recall when possible. Forecast scores and error bars remain scenario outputs until verified; the validation file is not automatically saved or shared. Only matching Crow City operating hours are assumed for Crow City. Other venues use illustrative schedule assumptions.
